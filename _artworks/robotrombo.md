RoboTrombo is a robotic trombone machine originally built as an attempt to create a MIDI-controlled instrument capable of playing arbitrary songs.

The mechanical parts of the instrument could be automated, but recreating the human interaction between lips, airflow, pressure and resonance proved much harder. What started as a music machine gradually became an over-engineered noise machine.

The project explores the awkward boundary between mechanical precision and the surprisingly complex physical skill involved in playing an acoustic instrument.
