A true random number generator built as an electronic sculpture.

The device uses naturally occurring background ionizing radiation as a source of entropy. An old Soviet Geiger tube detects individual events, and the timing between them is used to generate random numbers, displayed on an American Nixie tube.

The piece brings together Cold War-era components, radioactive decay and digital logic in a physical random-number generator.
