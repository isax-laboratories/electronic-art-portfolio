Cyclotone is a mechanical four-channel sequencer built around four Atari Punk Console synthesizers.

A rotating central mechanism distributes power to the individual synthesizers through physical contacts. The movement of the sculpture therefore becomes the sequencing mechanism itself, combining sound, electronics and mechanical motion into one object.
